Project Lightpath explores how software teams can move from raw security and engineering signals to decisions they can understand, verify, and act on.

Our repositories cover connected parts of that path: preparing trustworthy analysis context, understanding application behavior, prioritizing vulnerabilities, and generating high-fidelity tests. The goal is not a single opaque system. It is a set of components with explicit contracts, provenance, uncertainty, and human approval points.

## Contribute

The project is developed in the open. Browse the [Project Lightpath organization](https://github.com/project-lightpath), open an issue in the relevant repository, or contribute through its documented workflow.
